<a href="/people/#catherine-chen">Dr. Catherine Chen</a>'s study of how semantic relations are represented in the human brain has been accepted in Nature Human Behaviour. The paper has not yet appeared in print but you can find a preprint <a href="https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.02.19.706815">here</a>.

Participants answered relation-verification questions about over 1000 concept pairs (e.g., bicycle has-part wheel), and voxelwise encoding models were used to identify where and how each relation is represented. Semantic relations are encoded in the same bilateral temporal, parietal, and prefrontal regions as other semantic concepts, though most voxels are selective for only one specific relation.
